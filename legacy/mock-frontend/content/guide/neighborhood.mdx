## Know Your Neighborhood

Choosing the right neighborhood affects your commute, your budget, your social life and even how safe and comfortable you feel after classes.

### Istanbul Neighborhoods

- Kadıköy for a student-friendly social scene and nightlife
- Beşiktaş for central access and waterfront energy
- Beyoğlu and Taksim for a busy, tourist-heavy experience
- Üsküdar for a calmer atmosphere and Bosphorus views
- Fatih for history and lower prices in many areas

### Ankara Neighborhoods

- Kızılay for city-center access and transport
- Çankaya for safety and a more upscale atmosphere
- Bahçelievler for affordable student life
- Tunalı Hilmi for cafes and nightlife
- ODTÜ area for campus-oriented living

### İzmir Neighborhoods

- Alsancak for waterfront life and restaurants
- Bornova for affordability near Ege University
- Karşıyaka for a relaxed coastal feel
- Konak for the historic city center and bazaars

<GuideTip>Ask your ESN buddy which neighborhoods fit both your campus commute and your budget. Local context matters more than generic travel advice.</GuideTip>

### Safety Tips

- Stay aware in crowded transit hubs and nightlife areas
- Avoid walking alone in unfamiliar districts late at night
- Keep emergency numbers accessible
- Share your live location when going out if you are new to the city
- Register with your embassy if your home country recommends it
