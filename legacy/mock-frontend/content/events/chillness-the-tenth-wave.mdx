Get ready for **Chill'Ness: The Tenth Wave** ESN Türkiye’s flagship festival and this year’s **10th anniversary** edition (**“The Tenth Wave”**). Dates **21–24 May 2026**, **Eski Foça, İzmir** aligned with the official site at [chillness.esnturkey.org](https://chillness.esnturkey.org/).

There you’ll find the **countdown**, **activities** (day & night), **aftermovie**, **performers**, and **Reach Us** / contact flow maintained by the event team.

You can still edit images via `cardImage` / `heroImage` in the frontmatter, or add visuals in the body with Markdown (e.g. files under `public/`).

## What to expect

- Daytime **workshops** in dance, art, and more plus friendly competition: volleyball, football, darts, and games.
- Evenings turn into a **festival atmosphere** with DJ sets across electronic, techno, reggaeton, R&B, and more dance under the stars and meet people from across the network.

## What to bring

Pack for both comfort and fun! We recommend bringing:
- Comfortable clothes and shoes (for workshops and sports)
- Swimwear, towel, and sunscreen for the beach
- Jacket or hoodie for cool evenings
- Reusable water bottle & power bank
- Tent (if not renting), sleeping bag, and camping mat
- Personal hygiene items
- National/ESN flag, accessories, or costume for the parties!
- Cash/TL for small purchases (ATM may not always be nearby)
- Your best energy and Erasmus spirit!

## Participation options

- Choose your **arrival**: Thursday, Friday, or later.
- **Limited tent rentals** registered participants will be notified about availability by email.
- **Registration** is through ESN offices announced on Instagram: [@esnturkiye](https://www.instagram.com/esnturkiye/) and [@esnturkiye.events](https://www.instagram.com/esnturkiye.events/).

## Participation fees

- Transportation from İzmir: **85 EUR** / **4500 ₺**
- Transportation from İstanbul: **110 EUR** / **5800 ₺**
- Transportation from Ankara: **110 EUR** / **5800 ₺**
- Transportation from Eskişehir: **110 EUR** / **5800 ₺**
- Without transportation (Friday or later arrival): **70 EUR** / **3800 ₺**
- Without transportation (Thursday arrival): **80 EUR** / **4100 ₺**

## Tent rental

**20 EUR / 1000 TRY** valid for the whole stay; tents are for **one or two people** (subject to availability).
