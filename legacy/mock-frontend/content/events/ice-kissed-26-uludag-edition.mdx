Pack your warmest clothes and get ready for one of the coolest ESN Turkiye experiences: **Ice'Kissed 26 - Uludag Edition**.

This winter trip brought together Erasmus students and ESNers from all over Turkiye for a full weekend of snow, sports, and community.

## Program snapshot

- Event dates: **3-4 January 2026**
- Destination: **Uludag, Bursa**
- Focus: skiing, snowboarding, winter activities, and social program

## What was included

- Optional round-trip transportation
- Local transfers inside Uludag (for all participants)
- 1-night accommodation (double/triple rooms) at Gold Majesty Hotel, Bursa
- Open buffet dinner (Saturday)
- Open buffet breakfast (Sunday)
- Travel insurance
- English-speaking tour guide
- Free access to skiing area
- Discounts on ski and snowboard rental

## Participation prices

- With transportation from Istanbul: **5000 TRY (100 EUR)**
- With transportation from Izmir: **5200 TRY (105 EUR)**
- With transportation from Ankara: **5200 TRY (107 EUR)**
- Non-transportation: **4500 TRY (90 EUR)**

## Notes

- ESNcard was mandatory for Erasmus students.
- ESNcard fee in that period: **700 TRY / 15 EUR**.
- Extras such as skiing equipment and Iskender were announced separately.
