## Explore Türkiye on Weekends

Your Erasmus semester is the perfect time to explore Türkiye beyond your university city. Domestic travel is usually much more affordable than students expect.

<GuideTip>ESN sections often organize group trips on weekends. They are usually cheaper, simpler to plan, and a fast way to meet people.</GuideTip>

### Top Weekend Destinations

- Cappadocia for balloons, cave hotels, and a full weekend itinerary
- Pamukkale for travertines and a short escape from İzmir
- Ephesus for ancient history and day trips
- Antalya for beach weekends and old town walks
- Safranbolu for Ottoman architecture near Ankara
- Princes' Islands for a day trip from Istanbul

### Transportation Options

Türkiye has a wide range of student-friendly transport choices, from buses to domestic flights.

- Pegasus and AnadoluJet for budget flights
- Kamil Koç, Metro, and Pamukkale for intercity buses
- YHT high-speed train on selected routes
- BlaBlaCar for low-cost carpooling
- Car rental for group trips when split between friends

<GuideTip>Use obilet.com to compare intercity bus companies and prices in one place before booking.</GuideTip>

### Budget Tips

- Travel in groups of 4 to 6 when possible
- Book hostels early during high season
- Use museum and student discounts whenever available
- Eat at local lokanta restaurants to keep food costs low
- Plan overnight transport when it saves a hotel night
