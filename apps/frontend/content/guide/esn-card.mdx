## Your Magic Pass: ESNcard

The ESNcard is one of the most useful things you can get during your exchange. It unlocks travel discounts, event access and local perks across the ESN network.

<GuideTip>Get your ESNcard during Welcome Week if possible. You will use it immediately for local events and long-term travel deals.</GuideTip>

### How to Get Your ESNcard

- Visit your local ESN section office
- Bring your student ID and a passport-size photo if requested
- Pay the one-time card fee
- Activate the card in the ESNcard app
- Start checking current offers near your city

### Travel Discounts

- Ryanair discounts on selected flights
- FlixBus deals on European routes
- Hostelworld and car-rental partner offers
- Airline or bus partner campaigns that change during the year

### Local Discounts in Türkiye

Many local businesses work with ESN sections and the ESNcard platform, so the exact offers depend on where you study.

- Restaurant and cafe discounts
- Reduced prices for activities and tours
- Special deals at partner stores
- Gym and fitness promotions in some cities

### ESN Events Access

Your card often gives you discounted or priority access to ESN events, from welcome parties to group trips and larger national gatherings.
