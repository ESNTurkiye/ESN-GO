## Finding Your Home in Türkiye

Finding the right accommodation is one of the most important steps of your Erasmus journey. Whether you prefer a university dormitory, a shared apartment, or a private studio, Türkiye offers plenty of options for international students at different price ranges.

<GuideTip>Start your housing search at least 2 months before your arrival. The best options go fast, especially in popular cities like Istanbul and Ankara.</GuideTip>

### University Dormitories (KYK & Private)

KYK (Kredi ve Yurtlar Kurumu) is the government-run student dormitory system. It is usually the most affordable option, often including meals. Availability for exchange students can be limited, so contact your university's international office as early as possible.

- Apply through your university's International Relations Office
- KYK dorms usually include breakfast and dinner
- Private dorms cost more but usually offer more comfort and flexibility
- Most dorms provide Wi-Fi, laundry and study rooms
- Gender-separated floors are standard in many dorms

### Renting a Shared Apartment

Sharing an apartment is the most popular option among Erasmus students. It offers more independence and a stronger connection to local daily life.

<GuideTip>Never send money before visiting the apartment in person. Scams targeting international students are common, so always verify the landlord and the property first.</GuideTip>

- Sahibinden.com for the largest property listing pool
- Hepsiemlak.com for additional local listings
- Facebook groups like Erasmus housing groups in your city
- Your ESN section's housing assistance channels
- Your local buddy for apartment visits and translation help

### Rental Contract Essentials

When you find a place, sign a proper rental contract (kira sözleşmesi). If possible, ask for a bilingual contract or have someone explain the Turkish version clearly before you sign.

- Deposit is typically 1 to 2 months of rent
- Utilities are often separate from rent
- Internet setup may take a few days
- Register your address if your university or residence permit process requires it
- Keep copies of all documents and receipts
