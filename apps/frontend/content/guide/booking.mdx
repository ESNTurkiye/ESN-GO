## Booking Like a Local

From booking accommodation to buying event tickets, using the right Turkish platforms can save you money and help you avoid last-minute problems.

### Accommodation Booking

- Booking.com for short stays and hostels
- Airbnb for longer stays and monthly deals
- Jolly Tur and Tatilbudur for local travel packages
- Hotels.com for loyalty rewards on hotel bookings

### Event and Activity Tickets

Concerts, football matches and large city events often sell out fast. Booking early matters much more than many incoming students expect.

- Biletix for concerts, theatre and sports
- Passo for football match tickets
- Mobilet for events and activities
- Meetup for local interest groups
- Your ESN section pages for student-focused events

<GuideTip>If you want to watch football in Türkiye, check Passolig requirements first. Many stadium events cannot be booked without it.</GuideTip>

### Restaurant Reservations

In busy cities, popular restaurants can fill up quickly on weekends, especially in neighborhoods with strong student traffic.

- Google Maps listing links often work well for quick reservations
- Instagram direct messages are common for trendy restaurants
- Calling ahead is still very normal in Türkiye
- Delivery apps like Getir Yemek and Yemeksepeti are useful backup options
