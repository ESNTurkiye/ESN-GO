# Code of Conduct

> **"Ethical values are the foundation of trust; trust is the key to success."**  
> February 2025

This Code of Conduct is based on the official ESN Türkiye Code of Conduct and is intended to be fully aligned with it.  
It complements ESN Türkiye’s statutes, standing orders and policies and does **not** replace any existing legal or internal rules.

## 1. PURPOSE

The primary goal of ESN Türkiye is to create a respectful and safe environment that increases the inclusiveness of activities that can be attended by all volunteers, candidates, and guests.  

Events, trainings and meetings organised by ESN Türkiye must follow the highest ethical and professional standards so that **all participants** benefit from the organisation and feel safe and respected.  

All participants are expected to:

- behave **honestly**,  
- act with **respect** towards every other participant, and  
- uphold the values and image of ESN Türkiye at all times.

## 2. APPLICABILITY

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- any ESN Türkiye structure, body or section, and  
- any event or activity that is organised, co-organised, hosted or sponsored by ESN Türkiye or its sections.

It is valid for, but not limited to:

- trainings, seminars, workshops, conferences, meetings, exhibitions, side events and social events,  
- any gathering where the ESN name, logo or representation is used, whether fully or partially, and  
- any ESN-related **online activity** (e.g. social media, messaging platforms, emails, online meetings).

The Code applies to **all participants** at an activity. Anyone present in that environment – including participants, volunteers, organisers, guests or audience – implicitly undertakes to abide by this Code of Conduct.

This Code of Conduct:

- is **not** legal or prescriptive in nature,  
- complements the application of ESN Türkiye and ESN AISBL values, and  
- does **not** change any previously written rules.

It is always subordinate to the laws and cumulative legal documents of the country of residence.

## 3. PERSONS TO WHOM THIS CODE APPLIES

This Code of Conduct applies to any:

- individual,  
- organisation, or  
- group or gathering  

that partakes in any activity of ESN Türkiye.

It covers conduct at:

- all meetings and events mentioned by ESN Türkiye’s statutes, standing orders and event policies,  
- all ESN-related online activity, and  
- all external representation of ESN (for example, representation towards universities, partners or the public).

## 4. PROHIBITED CONDUCT

All individuals taking part in ESN Türkiye activities must act according to ESN values and work to facilitate an **inclusive** and **accessible** network.

Individuals should be:

- respectful in their communication,  
- constructive and formative in their feedback, and  
- mindful of the impact of their words and actions on others.

ESN Türkiye will **not tolerate** any instance of verbally or physically aggressive behaviour.

Any violation mentioned or implied in this Code should be reported to the designated **Person of Contact** (see Section 5) and will be dealt with according to the relevant procedure.

### 4.1 General definition

Prohibited conduct is **any inappropriate or undesirable behaviour** that a reasonable person cannot be expected to tolerate.

This includes, but is not limited to:

- insulting, humiliating or demeaning another person,  
- any form of harassment, and  
- any attack or discriminatory behaviour based on factors such as:
  - gender,  
  - gender identity or expression,  
  - sexual orientation,  
  - physical ability or disability,  
  - physical appearance,  
  - ethnicity or race,  
  - national origin,  
  - political affiliation,  
  - age, or  
  - role or experience within ESN Türkiye.

### 4.2 Sexual harassment

Sexual harassment is a **specific type** of prohibited behaviour.

Sexual harassment is any **unwanted behaviour of a sexual nature**. It can be:

- verbal,  
- non-verbal, or  
- physical,  

including written and electronic communication. It can occur:

- between people of the same or different genders, and  
- in any ESN-related context (offline or online).

Sexual harassment victimises a person and is strictly prohibited.

**Examples of sexual harassment include, but are not restricted to:**

- making derogatory or degrading remarks about someone’s sexual orientation or gender identity,  
- name-calling or using slurs with a gender or sexual connotation,  
- making sexual comments about appearance, clothing or body parts,  
- rating or commenting on a person’s sexuality,  
- repeatedly asking a person for dates or for sex,  
- staring at someone in a sexually suggestive manner,  
- unwelcome touching, including pinching, patting, rubbing or purposefully brushing up against a person,  
- making inappropriate sexual gestures (e.g. pelvic thrusts),  
- sharing sexual or vulgar anecdotes or jokes,  
- sending sexually suggestive communications in any format,  
- sharing or displaying sexually inappropriate images or videos in any format,  
- any attempted or actual sexual assault, including rape.

## 5. PERSONS OF CONTACT

All parties must be made aware of their rights and responsibilities under this Code of Conduct, and of **who** they can contact in case of a violation.

At ESN Türkiye events, the **Person(s) of Contact** can be:

- the principal facilitator(s) of the meeting,  
- the chairing team,  
- the Board of Auditors, or  
- a member of the National Board.

If you feel safe doing so, you should report any instance of:

- verbal or non-verbal discrimination,  
- verbal or physical harassment, or  
- bullying  

to a Person of Contact.

For violations perpetrated through online platforms, you may use an **anonymous incident report form** specifically created for Code of Conduct violations.

### 5.1 Actions by the Person of Contact

The Person of Contact must take the following actions, **in order and depending on the seriousness of the incident**:

1. **Remind** participants that they must adhere to the Code of Conduct.  
2. Have a **one‑on‑one discussion** with the offender to ensure they understand why their behaviour is inappropriate.  
3. **Hear both sides** of the story and, where appropriate, request an apology or a retraction of the behaviour.  
4. **Suspend the session** or insist that the offender leaves the remaining portions of it.  

Where possible, decisions should **not** be made by a single Person of Contact alone. Consultation with other relevant persons (e.g. another Board member or auditor) is encouraged.

If at any time the Person of Contact believes that there is a **risk of injury** to any person, they must:

- ensure that the location is safe for all participants, and  
- if necessary and feasible under local legislation, contact the appropriate public authorities.

All parties are required to maintain **confidentiality and discretion** about any information they become aware of during the mediation or complaint-handling process, in order to safeguard the privacy of those involved, except when:

- disclosure is required by law, or  
- it is necessary to protect someone’s safety.

Without permission, the Person of Contact may not reveal the identities of the parties involved.

## 6. COMPLAINT PROCESS

Participants who experience harassment at an ESN event **should** report the incident to:

- the event’s organiser, and/or  
- the appropriate security or safety authority.  

Participants who **witness** harassment are also strongly encouraged to report it.

Such reporting does **not** replace or limit:

- any applicable policies and procedures within the Erasmus Student Network, or  
- any procedures that may apply to other personnel or external parties.

The event organiser is expected to act appropriately in accordance with ESN Türkiye’s applicable statutes, rules and regulations.

**Examples of appropriate actions include, but are not limited to:**

- undertaking a fact‑finding exercise or internal investigation,  
- requesting the perpetrator to immediately stop the behaviour,  
- suspending or terminating the perpetrator’s access to the ESN event,  
- refusing registration of the perpetrator at future ESN events, or both,  
- conveying the complaint to any investigative or disciplinary authority with jurisdiction over the person accused of harassment,  
- conveying a report to the employer, university or entity with jurisdiction over the person accused of harassment for appropriate follow‑up action (this may apply, for example, to external representatives or International Office staff).

In accordance with the relevant legal framework, the allegedly harassed party may also seek assistance from other competent authorities, such as the **police**.

> A participant must **never** knowingly or intentionally misrepresent prohibited conduct in any way.

## 7. PROHIBITION OF RETALIATION

It is strictly forbidden to use:

- threats,  
- intimidation, or  
- any other form of retaliation  

against a participant who has:

- filed a complaint, or  
- supplied evidence or information in support of a complaint.

In line with its applicable laws, regulations and rules, ESN Türkiye or any other body in charge of an ESN event will take all reasonably necessary actions to **prevent and respond to retaliation**.

## 8. NOTIFICATIONS VIA FORM

Notifications related to violations of this Code can be made:

- via the online form: `https://forms.esnturkey.org/coc-violations`, or  
- by sending an email to the National Board of Auditors’ email address: `auditors@esnturkey.org`.

The notification should include, as far as possible:

- the name and contact information of the reporting person,  
- the person against whom the violation occurred,  
- where and when it happened,  
- who carried it out, and  
- any available evidence and witnesses.

As stated under **Applicability**, the same procedure applies to violations that:

- occur outside ESN events, but  
- still fall within the scope of ESN Türkiye.
